A newspaper front page for Sunday 22 November 2026: **24 stories** across
6 regions and 9 fields, a UTC timeline of the day, a region breakdown, a
confidence label on every story (CONFIRMED / OFFICIAL / REPORTED / CLAIM /
FORECAST), and a DATE CHECK box listing what was deliberately left out.

The four leads: COP31 ends with no deal in Antalya; a Russian warship is
sunk at Benghazi; Democrats clinch 258 House seats; Iran strikes US bases
in Bahrain. Every story is dated 22 November 2026 with its sources named
(see NOTES.md for the source list).

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "News Image" bounty, $100 in USDC.

- `main.py` — entry point (stdlib only): copies `entry.png` to the output dir.
- `compose_poster.py` — draws the poster with Pillow from the story data.
- `entry.png` — the poster, 1024x1024, under 1 MiB.
